## Fellowships & Scholarships

1. **DST INSPIRE Senior Research Fellowship (2021–2024):** Awarded by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India, under the INSPIRE Fellowship programme to support doctoral research in Physics.

2. **DST INSPIRE Junior Research Fellowship (2019–2021):** Awarded by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India, under the INSPIRE Fellowship programme for pursuing doctoral research.

3. **DST INSPIRE Scholarship for Higher Education (2013–2018):** Awarded by the Department of Science and Technology (DST), Government of India, under the INSPIRE Scholarship for Higher Education (SHE) programme to support undergraduate and postgraduate studies in basic sciences.


## National-Level Examinations Qualified

1. **UGC -- NET in Physical Sciences (2019):** Qualified the University Grants Commission National Eligibility Test (UGC-NET) in Physical Sciences, a national-level examination for determining eligibility for lectureship in Indian universities and colleges.

2. **Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering — GATE, Physics (2019):** Qualified the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) in Physics, a national-level examination conducted for postgraduate admissions and research opportunities.

3. **Joint Admission Test for M.Sc. — IIT-JAM, Physics (2016):** Qualified the Joint Admission Test for M.Sc. (IIT-JAM) in Physics, conducted by the Indian Institutes of Technology for admission to postgraduate and integrated research programmes.
