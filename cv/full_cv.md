<section class="cv-section">

<div class="cv-label">
About Me
</div>

<div class="cv-content">

<p>
Postdoctoral Fellow at Guangdong Technion-Israel Institute of Technology
with a PhD in Physics from Aligarh Muslim University. My research lies at
the interface of non-equilibrium statistical physics, complex systems,
and applied mathematics, with expertise in self-organized criticality,
sandpile models, stochastic processes, and nonlinear dynamical systems.
My current work focuses on the mathematical and computational analysis
of directed Abelian sandpile models, renormalization-group methods,
stochastic processes with memory, and emergent phenomena in driven
non-equilibrium systems. I have extensive experience in analytical
modeling, large-scale numerical simulations, and scientific computing
using FORTRAN, C/C++, Python, and MATLAB.
</p>

</div>

</section>
